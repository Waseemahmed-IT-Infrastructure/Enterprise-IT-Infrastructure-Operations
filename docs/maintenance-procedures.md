# Infrastructure Maintenance Procedures

1. Identify the systems and dependencies in scope.
2. Review vendor guidance and prerequisites.
3. Obtain change approval and confirm a maintenance window.
4. Verify backup and recovery readiness.
5. Perform testing where practical.
6. Execute the approved maintenance plan.
7. Check services, network connectivity, monitoring, and logs.
8. Record the outcome and any follow-up tasks.

Do not apply patches, restart systems, or change configurations without authorization.
