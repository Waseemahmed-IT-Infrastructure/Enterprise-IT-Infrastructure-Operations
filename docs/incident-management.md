# Incident Management Workflow

1. Detect and log the incident.
2. Determine impact, urgency, and affected service.
3. Assign priority according to organizational policy.
4. Gather read-only diagnostics and monitoring evidence.
5. Escalate to the appropriate support team if needed.
6. Implement only authorized recovery actions.
7. Verify service restoration with affected stakeholders.
8. Record timeline, resolution, and follow-up actions.

## Example (fictional)

A lab server reports low free disk space. Verify the alert, review disk utilization, identify growth, and raise an approved capacity request. Do not delete files without authorization.
